About Creative Studios

The stories worth remembering

Creative Studios is a photography and filmmaking studio based in Nellore, capturing weddings with an eye for the moments that matter. We look for the quiet glances, unexpected laughter, and in-between moments that make each celebration uniquely yours.

Our approach is simple — to let the day unfold naturally and create photographs and films that bring you back to how it truly felt.
