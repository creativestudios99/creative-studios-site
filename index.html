The Studio

Two artists. One shared eye for the unrepeatable moment.

Creative Studios is a husband-and-wife photography and videography team, working across the country to document weddings with a documentary eye and a painter's patience for light. We shoot every wedding as though it's the only one we'll ever get to tell.

No forced poses, no rushed timelines — just the day, as it actually happened, kept for the rest of your life.
